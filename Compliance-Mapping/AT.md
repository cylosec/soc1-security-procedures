# NIST 800-53 Control Family: AT – Awareness and Training

## Summary
Ensures personnel are aware of security risks, policies, and responsibilities, and are adequately trained to carry them out.

## ISO/IEC 27001 Mapping
- **A.6.3** – Information security awareness, education, and training

## SOX Relevance
- **Section 302/404**: Requires executives to certify that controls (including training) are in place and effective.

## SOC 1–2 Analyst Action Items
- Monitor and report on completion of annual security training
- Track phishing simulation results
- Identify users who repeatedly fail awareness tests
- Support HR in enforcing mandatory training policies

## Detection/Audit Examples
- SIEM or HR system export showing % of users trained
- Phishing simulation logs showing who clicked malicious links
- Audit report confirming users completed onboarding security training
