# NIST 800-53 Control Family: RA – Risk Assessment

## Summary
Involves identifying risks to organizational operations and information systems.

## ISO/IEC 27001 Mapping
- **A.5.4** – Risk management process
- **A.5.5** – Inventory of information and assets

## SOX Relevance
- **Section 404**: Risk identification is foundational to internal control evaluations

## SOC 1–2 Analyst Action Items
- Assist with risk scoring of vulnerabilities or threats
- Provide input into asset criticality assessments
- Support prioritization of alerts based on business impact

## Detection/Audit Examples
- Weekly threat intelligence review for new CVEs
- Mapping vulnerabilities to asset classification levels
