# NIST 800-53 Control Family: IR – Incident Response

## Summary
Ensures organizations can detect, respond to, and recover from security incidents.

## ISO/IEC 27001 Mapping
- **A.5.24** – Information security incident management planning
- **A.5.25** – Assessment and decision on information security events

## SOX Relevance
- **Sections 302/404**: Executive certification requires disclosure and response to material incidents

## SOC 1–2 Analyst Action Items
- Triage alerts and escalate as per SOP
- Document incidents in JIRA or case tracking system
- Tag alerts with MITRE ATT&CK TTPs
- Participate in tabletop IR exercises

## Detection/Audit Examples
- High-severity Wazuh alert linked to suspicious PowerShell execution (T1059)
- JIRA ticket with timeline, root cause, and remediation
